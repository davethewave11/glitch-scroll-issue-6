Issue 6: The Story of Daytona

Pusha T vs The Industry – The Timeline Scroll

06:27 – Funk Flex Freestyle

This throwback freestyle showed the razor-sharp wordplay that made Pusha a feared emcee. His bars sliced over a classic beat with no safety net — just surgical rap in its rawest form.

17:34 – Round One: Infrared

“It was written like Nas, but it came from Quentin.” With that line, Pusha fired the first shot of the most surgical beef in modern rap. Infrared wasn’t loud — it was calculated.

19:51 – Round Two: Duppy Freestyle

Drake responded fast with Duppy, flexing style, emotion, and arrogance. It was a strong reply — until the next bomb dropped.

22:26 – Round Three: The Story of Adidon

History. “You are hiding a child.” Pusha exposed the untouchable. The blackface photo. The family secrets. Hip Hop stopped — and watched.

30:10 – Puff Daddy Presents The L

Pusha didn’t just win — he delivered the L with full ceremony. This meme-ified moment is still a cultural timestamp on Drake’s resume.

30:58 – Outro: “We Make Good Music”

The final stroke. Instead of bragging, he simply stated it — Good Music doesn’t lose. Scroll closed. Mic dropped.
